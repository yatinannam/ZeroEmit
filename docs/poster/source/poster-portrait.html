ZeroEmit

Charge your EV when India's grid is cleaner.
Live grid dataAll 5 regional grids of India, every state and ~250 cities
Smart time slotsBest 2-hour windows for today and tomorrow
RewardsEco points, streaks, badges and a monthly leaderboard
The problem. The same EV charge can be far dirtier at 8 pm than at noon, because India's grid leans on coal in the evening peak. Drivers can't see this.
Our solution. ZeroEmit reads regional grid carbon intensity, recommends the cleanest hours to plug in, sends reminders, and rewards drivers who shift their charging.
